Design, architecture, and development of a mobile-friendly, fully responsive website optimized for performance, user experience, and easy navigation. Ongoing website maintenance, updates, and technical support.

Deliverables: 8 templates, 2 home page design concepts, 2 design rounds, 40 pages, WordPress (Headless CMS) with Next.js front-end, up to 4 forms.

### Blog Development

Development of a customized blog platform including content and data entry. Deliverables: 3 templates, 2 design rounds, 10 pages.

### Performance and Security

Website performance, security, and infrastructure optimization including Core Web Vitals improvements, CDN and caching setup, SSL and firewall configuration, backups, monitoring and load testing, optional VAPT support.

Deliverables: Performance Optimization, Security Measures, Testing Protocols, VAPT all Included. Tool: BurpSuite.

### Website Hosting

Amazon Cloud hosting setup with SSL and CDN integration. Ongoing hosting management and support. Uptime SLA 99.95 percent.

### Content Development

Style guide defining tone, voice, and messaging style. Website content writing focused on clear communication and structured content. Deliverables: Style Guide, Sample Content, 40 pages, 20 snippets.

### Search Engine Optimization

SEO setup including technical analysis, keyword strategy, on-page optimization. Ongoing SEO activities including keyword monitoring, content marketing, link building, webmaster tool management.

Deliverables: SEO Technical Audit, SEO Keyword Strategy, 40 Customized Title Meta Tags, 20 H1 Tags, 20 Content Optimizations, Webmaster Account Setup, Internal Linking, Schema Implementation, Monthly Search Console Management, Bi-Annual Link Profile Audits, Monthly reports.

### Paid Article and Content Submissions

Researching and identifying high Domain Authority (DA 50+) third-party websites, coordinating article submission opportunities, developing and submitting optimized articles.

### Local Business Listings

Setup and optimization of local business listings across platforms including Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places for Business, and optional Yandex, Yelp, Foursquare.

### AEYEPULSE

AEYEPULSE enables monitoring of brand mentions, sentiment analysis, and evaluation of brand positioning within AI-generated content. Package: Growth.

### Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

GEO ensures that a brand's content across online sources is represented within AI-driven answers. Includes Technical Readiness and Content Structure Audit, monthly authority building through question-based blog content, listicles, comparison guides, brand mentions, and co-citations.

Deliverables: Intelligent Prompt and Query Research, up to 10 Prompt Optimizations, up to 10 FAQ Recommendations, Structured Content and Onsite Optimization, Technical SEO Audit for GEO, monthly AI Authority Trust and GEO Reputation, Report and Monitoring.

### GTM and Google Analytics

Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics setup for accurate visitor and conversion tracking. GA4 data monitoring and Looker Studio report optimization.

Deliverables: GA4/GTM Audit and Setup, 1 Looker Studio Report, 1 GA4 Training, optional Server-side Tracking via Stape, optional Meta Conversions API Gateway, optional Cookie Consent Management.

### Social Media Optimization (SMO)

Social media setup including concept creation, creative direction, and profile audit and optimization. Monthly management covering content creation, posting schedules, and performance reporting.

Deliverables: up to 2 platforms, Facebook and Instagram included, LinkedIn and TikTok optional. Core package: 10 posts and 2 stories per month.

### Social Media Advertising (SMA)

Social media advertising strategy, creative development, campaign structure, campaign setup, conversion tracking. Ongoing campaign management.

Deliverables: up to 2 platforms, Facebook and Instagram included. Campaign Types: Post Boosting, Conversion Ads, Website Traffic Ads, Reach Campaigns, Lead Campaigns. Visual Assets: 4 single images, 2 carousels, 1 video.

### Pay per Click Marketing (PPC)

PPC strategy formulation including target audience identification, keyword research, ad copy creation, creative development, campaign structuring, campaign setup, and conversion tracking.

Deliverables: up to 2 channels and 4 mediums. Channels: Google Ads and DV360 included. Mediums: Search/RLSA, Display/DRM, Pmax Campaigns, Video/Video Remarketing. Visual Assets: 2 HTML5 banner sets, 2 Static banner sets.

### Blog Post Creation and Management

Blog content guidelines and style guides. Ongoing topic research and blog article creation. Monthly: 4 blog articles.

### PaymentsEye - Web Based Invoicing System

Customer invoicing module for charging offline sales through online payment links. Setup includes customized messaging and integration with up to 2 payment gateways. Integrations: Sampath Bank PayCrop and Global Payments Cybersource included.
