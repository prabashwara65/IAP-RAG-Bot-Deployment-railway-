## Entitlement

Every full-time employee receives twenty-one working days of paid annual leave in each calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on contracted hours.

## Requesting Leave

Annual leave must be requested at least ten working days before the first day of absence. The reporting manager approves or declines the request within three working days.

## Carry Over

A maximum of five unused annual leave days may be carried into the following calendar year. Carried days expire on the last day of March.
